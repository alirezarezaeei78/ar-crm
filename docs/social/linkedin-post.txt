Retail teams manage more than leads. They manage the relationship between a customer, a purchase, a product, and the next conversation.

I’m open-sourcing AR CRM — a retail-focused CRM built to bring those pieces into one clear workspace.

What’s inside:
• Customer profiles with purchase history, lifetime value, loyalty points, and notes
• Visual sales pipelines with weighted forecasts
• Orders, product catalogs, inventory, and reorder alerts
• Team task boards and automated welcome, follow-up, and restock tasks
• Customer segments and consent-aware campaign planning
• Sales dashboards, store comparisons, and CSV exports

Built with Next.js, React, and TypeScript, with responsive layouts and 19 browser integration checks.

It’s MIT licensed, includes fictional sample data, and runs locally without API keys or an external database.

This release is a working local demo and a foundation to build on. Production authentication, multi-user storage, and email/SMS delivery still need integration.
