Meet AR CRM — an open-source workspace built for retail.

Swipe through the CRM: business overview, customer profiles, sales pipeline, and product catalog.

• Know your customers: purchase history, loyalty, lifetime value, and notes
• Move sales forward: visual pipelines, weighted forecasts, and team tasks
• Connect retail operations: products, orders, inventory, and reorder alerts
• Plan smarter: customer segments, consent-aware campaigns, reports, and workflow automations

Built with Next.js, React, and TypeScript. MIT licensed. Includes fictional sample data, build instructions, and 19 browser integration checks. Runs locally without API keys or an external database.

This release is a working local demo. Production authentication, multi-user storage, and email/SMS delivery still need integration.

Code + setup guide:
https://github.com/alirezarezaeei78/ar-crm

Try it, explore the workflows, and contribute. Practical retail feedback is welcome.
